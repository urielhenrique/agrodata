# Research Question

## Primary Question

How can official agricultural and climate datasets be integrated into a
reproducible municipal-level analytical dataset for studying the relationship
between agricultural production and climatic variability in Minas Gerais?

## Motivation

Agricultural production is influenced by multiple environmental and
socioeconomic factors. Climate is one of the relevant dimensions, particularly
precipitation and temperature.

However, agricultural statistics and meteorological observations are
typically distributed across different official data systems, with different
spatial, temporal, and structural characteristics.

This study investigates a reproducible method for integrating these sources
at the municipal level.

## Secondary Questions

### Spatial

How should meteorological stations be associated with municipal territories
without relying on station names or manually defined municipality mappings?

### Temporal

How can higher-frequency meteorological observations be transformed into
annual indicators compatible with agricultural production statistics?

### Quality

Which quality-control procedures are necessary before meteorological
observations can be incorporated into agricultural analyses?

### Reproducibility

How can the provenance of an analytical observation be preserved from the
original official source through the transformation pipeline?

### Analytical

What statistical and spatial associations can be observed between climatic
variables and agricultural production?

## Hypothesis

H1:

Climatic variability is associated with variations in agricultural production
and/or productivity.

The hypothesis will be evaluated through statistical association rather than
causal inference.
